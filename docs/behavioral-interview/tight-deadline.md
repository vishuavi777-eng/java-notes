# Tight Deadline

## Question

Tell me about a time you worked under a tight deadline.

## Main Answer

In previous projects, tight deadlines came during feature releases, mobile app updates, admin panel changes, or automation requirements. In the nutrition and healthcare domain, some features were needed quickly because they directly affected customers, dietitians, or support teams.

When there was a tight deadline, I first tried to understand the most important requirement and break the work into smaller tasks. I focused on the core working flow first, then handled validations, edge cases, and improvements. If something needed more time, I communicated it clearly instead of hiding the delay.

For example, in customer support call automation, the important part was correct call scheduling and assignment. I focused first on making the allocation logic reliable, then improved other details after the main flow was working.

This taught me how to prioritize, communicate clearly, and deliver practical solutions under pressure.

## Key Points to Remember

- Prioritize important work.
- Break task into smaller parts.
- Communicate clearly.
- Focus on working core flow first.
- Do not promise unrealistic timelines.

## Short Version

During tight deadlines, I break work into smaller tasks and focus on the most important flow first. I also communicate risks clearly. This helped me deliver features like automation logic, admin changes, and mobile app updates in a practical way.

## Interview Tip

Show that you can work under pressure without ignoring quality completely.
