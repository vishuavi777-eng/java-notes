# Leadership and Ownership

## Question

Tell me about a time you took ownership.

## Main Answer

In my previous work, I took ownership of automation-related features and full application flows. One example is customer support call automation. Earlier, calls like weight check calls, motivation calls, birthday calls, feedback calls, and follow-up calls were handled manually.

I worked on understanding the business requirement, creating allocation logic, handling database flow, and making sure the system reduced manual work. This required understanding not only code, but also how the support team worked.

I also took ownership of maintaining and improving existing modules, fixing production issues, and supporting mobile app and admin panel workflows.

This experience helped me become more responsible for complete feature flow, not only one small task.

## Key Points to Remember

- Use automation project as example.
- Mention full flow understanding.
- Mention business impact.
- Mention maintenance and production support.
- Show responsibility without exaggeration.

## Short Version

I took ownership of automation features like customer support call allocation. I worked on requirement understanding, backend logic, database flow, and system improvement. This reduced manual work and helped me understand complete feature ownership.

## Interview Tip

Ownership means understanding business impact, not only writing code.
