# Notice Period

## Question

What is your notice period?

## Main Answer

My notice period will depend on my current company policy and release process. I will try to complete a proper handover and join as early as possible.

If there is flexibility from both sides, I can discuss and plan the joining date accordingly.

## Key Points to Remember

- Be honest.
- Mention company policy.
- Show willingness to join early if possible.
- Do not make false commitments.
- Keep answer short.

## Short Version

My notice period depends on my current company policy. I will complete proper handover and try to join as early as possible based on mutual agreement.

## Interview Tip

Do not promise an impossible joining date. Keep it realistic.
