# Strengths and Weaknesses

## Question

What are your strengths and weaknesses?

## Main Answer

One of my strengths is practical problem solving. I have worked on real business systems like diet automation, customer support call automation, mobile apps, backend APIs, admin panels, and database workflows. Because of this, I can understand a business problem and convert it into a technical solution.

Another strength is ownership. I try to understand the complete flow, not only my assigned part. For example, when working on automation systems, I had to understand user data, business rules, backend logic, database flow, and admin usage.

My weakness is that earlier I was more focused on completing features quickly, and sometimes I did not document the technical flow in detail. I have improved this by writing clearer notes, breaking logic into smaller parts, and focusing more on clean backend architecture.

## Key Points to Remember

- Strength: practical problem solving.
- Strength: ownership and full flow understanding.
- Weakness should be realistic but not harmful.
- Show improvement.
- Do not say fake weakness like "I work too hard".

## Short Version

My strength is practical problem solving and ownership. I can understand business requirements and convert them into working systems. I have done this in diet automation, call automation, APIs, admin panels, and mobile apps.

One area I improved is documentation and structured backend design. Earlier I focused more on fast delivery, but now I am improving clean architecture, notes, and better technical structure.

## Interview Tip

For weakness, always mention what you are doing to improve it.
