# Salary Expectation

## Question

What is your salary expectation?

## Main Answer

I am open to discussing salary based on the role, responsibilities, company standards, and overall opportunity. My main focus is to work in a good Java / Spring Boot or backend role where I can contribute my experience and continue growing technically.

Based on my experience and the role expectations, I would expect a fair offer as per market standards.

## Key Points to Remember

- Stay professional.
- Do not sound rigid too early.
- Mention role and responsibility.
- Keep answer short.
- Avoid giving unnecessary personal details.

## Short Version

I am open to discussing salary based on the role, responsibilities, and company standards. My focus is on a good Java / Spring Boot or backend opportunity where I can contribute and grow.

## Interview Tip

If interviewer asks for a number, give a realistic range based on your current salary, experience, and market research.
