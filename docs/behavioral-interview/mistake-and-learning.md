# Mistake and Learning

## Question

Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned.

## Main Answer

One mistake I made earlier was focusing more on completing the feature quickly and less on documenting the technical flow or edge cases clearly. In automation systems, business rules can become complex, and if the flow is not documented properly, future changes become harder.

I learned that when business logic is complex, clean structure and notes are important. Now I try to break logic into smaller parts, use clearer naming, document important rules, and think about edge cases before implementation.

This learning is also helping me now while improving Java and Spring Boot. I am focusing more on layered architecture, DTOs, validation, exception handling, and clean backend design.

## Key Points to Remember

- Pick a realistic mistake.
- Do not mention a dangerous mistake.
- Explain what you learned.
- Show improvement.
- Connect to current backend learning.

## Short Version

Earlier, I sometimes focused more on fast delivery and less on documenting complex technical flow. I learned that for automation and backend logic, clean structure, clear notes, and edge case handling are important. Now I focus more on structured design and maintainable code.

## Interview Tip

Do not say "I never made mistakes". A realistic learning answer is better.
