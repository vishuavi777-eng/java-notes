# Conflict with Team

## Question

Tell me about a time you had a conflict with a team member.

## Main Answer

In my previous projects, sometimes there were differences in understanding requirements, especially when business teams, dietitians, and developers were involved. For example, in diet automation work, business rules could change frequently, and different people had different expectations from the system.

In such situations, I tried to avoid arguing directly. I first understood the actual requirement and then discussed the technical impact. If something was risky or needed more time, I explained it with examples, like how it could affect existing diet rules, API flow, or user data.

Usually, after discussing clearly, we were able to agree on a practical solution. I learned that clear communication and requirement confirmation are very important before implementation.

## Key Points to Remember

- Do not blame team members.
- Focus on communication.
- Explain requirement clarity.
- Mention technical impact.
- End with learning.

## Short Version

I faced requirement differences during automation and diet system work. I handled it by understanding the actual requirement, explaining the technical impact, and discussing a practical solution with the team. This helped avoid confusion and improved delivery.

## Interview Tip

Keep the answer calm. Do not make it sound like a personal conflict.
